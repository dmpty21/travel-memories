Atlas

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 1, 2026

The short version

Atlas stores your travel memories — places, trips, and recommendations — in your own personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We don't run our own servers, we don't collect analytics, we don't show ads, and we don't share or sell your data to anyone. Nobody at Atlas can see your data; it's private to your iCloud account, the same way Notes or Photos data is.

What we collect

Everything Atlas stores is content you create yourself while using the app:

  • Your name — entered once, when you first open the app.
  • Photos — only the specific photos you choose to attach to a place, picked through Apple's standard photo picker. Atlas never gets broad access to your photo library.
  • Places and trips — countries, cities, trip dates, and trip type (business or leisure) that you enter.
  • Recommendations — names, notes, ratings, and favorite status for restaurants, museums, parks, activities, and coffee shops you add. If you look up a place using Apple Maps within the app, its address and coordinates are saved with that entry.

Atlas does not access your device's location (GPS). Any coordinates stored in the app come from a place you explicitly searched for, not from tracking your device.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored using Apple's CloudKit, in the private iCloud database tied to your own Apple ID. It syncs automatically across your own devices and is not accessible to us, to other Atlas users, or to any third party. If iCloud sync isn't available on your device, Atlas falls back to storing your data locally on that device only.

Third parties

Atlas does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only outside service involved is Apple's own CloudKit and MapKit, used solely to sync your data and to look up places you search for.

Your control over your data

You can edit or delete any place, trip, or recommendation directly in the app at any time. Because your data lives in your personal iCloud account, you can also manage or remove it through your device's iCloud settings. Deleting the app removes any data that hadn't synced to iCloud.

Children's privacy

Atlas is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
