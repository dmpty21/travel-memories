Where is my data stored?

Everything you add to Atlas — places, trips, photos, and recommendations — is stored in your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. It syncs automatically across your devices signed into the same Apple ID. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My data isn't syncing between devices

Try the following:

  • Make sure you're signed into the same iCloud account on all your devices, under Settings → [your name].
  • Check that iCloud Drive is turned on for your device, under Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • Make sure you have an internet connection — sync happens automatically once you're online.
  • If it still doesn't sync after a few minutes, try closing and reopening Atlas.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual places, trips, or recommendations directly in the app by swiping to delete or using the delete option on their detail screen. Deleting the app itself removes anything that hasn't finished syncing to iCloud; data already in iCloud can be managed from your device's iCloud settings.
